Dr. Sumeet Mankar

Plant Genomics & Quantitative Genetics
Postdoctoral Associate · Schnable Lab · University of Nebraska–Lincoln

I work at the intersection of plant genetics, genomics, and data-driven discovery — applying statistical and computational approaches to understand the genetic architecture of complex traits in crops. My research spans GWAS, QTL mapping, transcriptome-wide association studies (TWAS), and expression QTL analyses across maize, sorghum, soybean, rice, wheat, hemp, and ryegrass, with the goal of connecting molecular variation to agronomic outcomes.

Research

Key areas of active investigation at the Schnable Lab and beyond

🌽
GWAS & QTL Mapping in Cereals

Genome-wide association and quantitative trait loci analyses in maize, sorghum, and rice to identify genomic regions underlying yield, stress tolerance, phenology, and root architecture.

GWAS QTL Maize Sorghum Rice
🧬
Transcriptome-Wide Association Studies

TWAS, eQTL, and splicing QTL (sQTL) analyses to connect gene expression variation to complex trait loci, using population-scale RNA-seq data from hundreds of genotypes.

TWAS eQTL sQTL RNA-seq Expression Genetics
📊
Genomic Prediction & Breeding Analytics

Developing and benchmarking genomic prediction models for agronomic traits, with applications in accelerating crop improvement and estimating breeding value across diverse germplasm.

Genomic Prediction BLUP Machine Learning Breeding Design
🌿
Comparative Multi-Crop Genomics

Cross-species genomic analyses spanning soybean, wheat, hemp, and ryegrass to identify conserved regulatory elements, trait loci, and mechanisms underlying crop adaptation.

Comparative Genomics Pangenomics Soybean Wheat Hemp
🌱
Root & Stress Phenomics

Integrating high-throughput phenotyping — including root imaging, NIR spectroscopy, UAV imaging, and micro-CT — with genomic data to dissect drought tolerance and root architecture variation.

Root Architecture Drought Tolerance NIR Spectroscopy UAV Phenotyping
⚙️
Bioinformatics Pipeline Development

Building reproducible, scalable workflows for variant calling, transcriptomic processing, association testing, and multi-omics integration on HPC and cloud platforms.

Snakemake Nextflow HPC AWS Open Source
